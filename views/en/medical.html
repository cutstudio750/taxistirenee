Which trips

When an approved taxi can be covered.

Your doctor decides whether your condition justifies seated transport by taxi and writes the prescription. The most common situations:

  • Hospital staysAdmission, discharge or transfer, including day surgery.
  • Long-term conditionsTrips linked to a long-term condition (ALD), when your health does not allow you to travel alone or by public transport.
  • Series of treatmentsDialysis, chemotherapy, radiotherapy, rehabilitation: repeated trips for the same treatment.
  • Work accidents, occupational illnessTrips related to the prescribed care.
Before the day

What to have with you.

The prescription

Issued by your doctor before the trip, except in an emergency. It states the mode of transport.

The Carte Vitale

Or your up-to-date certificate of entitlement. Remember your top-up insurance card too.

The appointment letter

Or the admission form, with the department and the time of the appointment.

Prior approval

Required for some trips (long distance, distant series of treatments). Your doctor will tell you.

Depending on your situation, a fixed medical deductible per trip may remain payable. Reimbursement rules are set by the Assurance Maladie: if in doubt, ask your health insurance office or see ameli.fr (in French).

Questions

Medical transport: the answers.

What if I do not have the prescription yet?

You can send your request now and indicate that you will receive it. It must be issued before the trip, except in an emergency. Without a prescription, the trip cannot be covered by the Assurance Maladie; it is still possible as a regular ride, at your own expense.

Can someone come with me?

Depending on your situation, for example for a child or someone who needs help, a companion can travel with you. Mention it when booking so the seat is planned.

What if my appointment runs late?

Let the driver know by phone or message at the end of the appointment: the return is moved accordingly.

Do medical trips count towards the loyalty card?

No. As these trips are covered by the Assurance Maladie, they do not earn the loyalty reward. They remain visible in your client area, with their invoices.
