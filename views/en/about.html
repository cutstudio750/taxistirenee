About

Taxi Saint-Irénée is a craftsman's business. Since 2014, the driver has driven every client himself. The name is written like a signature: it is his personal commitment.

The story

Twelve years of Lyon, seen from the driver's seat.

Taxi Saint-Irénée started in 2014 with a simple idea: make the taxi a service you can count on, not a gamble on an app's availability.

Over the years, the same clients have come back: for the airport, for months of treatment sessions, to take a parent to hospital, to welcome a guest at the station. What brings them back is not only the car. It is knowing who will come, and knowing he will be there.

The approval by the French health insurance follows the same logic. Accompanying a patient takes punctuality, patience and real attention to people: that is the heart of the job.

What does not change

Four rules, since day one.

01

Answer in person

On the phone, the driver answers. An online request is always followed by a call.

02

Never promise what cannot be kept

If a time is not possible, you know straight away, with an alternative. No automatic confirmation that turns out to be wrong on the morning itself.

03

Be there early

Arriving ahead of time is part of the service, especially when a train, a flight or a treatment will not wait.

04

Respect everyone's time

Some clients want to talk about Lyon, others to work or rest. The conversation follows your pace.

The name

Saint-Irénée, like the neighbourhood on the hill.

Saint-Irénée is a neighbourhood of Lyon's 5th arrondissement, on the heights of Fourvière, between old stones and a view over the whole city.

The brand colours come from it: black for discretion, sand and taupe for the golden stone of Lyon's façades, cream for the leather of a well-kept interior.
