Medical transport

Your medical appointments, covered according to your prescription.

Taxi Saint-Irénée is approved by the French health insurance (CPAM). With a medical transport prescription, your trip can be reimbursed and, when the conditions are met, you do not pay upfront.

Dialysis, chemotherapy or radiotherapy sessions: regular trips are scheduled in one go, with the same driver from the first appointment to the last.

Questions

Before you book.

More answers on the contact page, or by phone on {{PHONE_INTL}}.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You can book with just your name and phone number. An account saves your addresses, keeps your invoices and counts your rides on the loyalty card.No. Your name and phone number are enough: the request is sent by WhatsApp or email, and the driver calls you back to confirm.

Is my booking confirmed as soon as I send the form?

No. The form sends a request. The driver calls you back to confirm his availability, the time and the pick-up point. The ride is confirmed at that moment.

Can I book in English?

Yes. Requests sent from the English version are marked as such, so the driver knows before he calls you. If you would rather write, WhatsApp or email work just as well.

How much does a ride cost?

Taxi fares are regulated by the prefecture and shown on the meter: they depend on distance, time and day. The website gives no automatic estimate; the driver gives you the details when he calls to confirm.

How many passengers can you take?

The vehicle has 7 seats, driver included: up to 6 passengers. State the number of passengers and pieces of luggage in your request so the pick-up is planned accordingly.

How do I pay?

On board, by card or in cash. Nothing is charged when you book. An invoice is issued for every ride.
