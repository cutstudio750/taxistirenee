Questions

What people often ask us.

How does an online booking work?

You send a request with the route, the date and your details. The driver calls you back to confirm: the ride is only booked after this call. If you have an account, its status changes to "confirmed" in your client area.

Why is there no price estimate on the site?

A taxi fare depends on the regulated rate in force, the time, the day and actual traffic. Rather than an approximate figure, the driver gives you the exact details on the phone.

How do I pay?

On board, by card or in cash. For covered medical transport, no upfront payment is needed when the conditions are met.

Where can I find my invoices?

With an account, all your invoices are in your client area, to view or save as PDF. Without an account, the invoice is emailed to you if you gave your address.You are given an invoice at the end of every ride. On request, it can also be emailed to you.

How do I cancel or change a ride?

From your client area, or by phone.By phone, WhatsApp or email. For a same-day change, call {{PHONE_INTL}} directly.

How many passengers and how much luggage?

The vehicle has 7 seats, driver included: up to 6 passengers. State the number of passengers and suitcases when booking: for a group or a heavily packed holiday departure, the driver checks with you that everyone and everything will fit comfortably.
